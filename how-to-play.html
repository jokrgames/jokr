How to play JOKR

One minute, one real game. Get rid of all your cards — and don’t be the one left holding the Joker.

The rules

  • Everyone gets cards. 53 in all — a normal deck plus one Joker — dealt between 4 and 7 players.
  • Pairs go by themselves. Two cards of the same number, any suits, are thrown away at once, face up.
  • On your turn, take a hidden card from another player. If it makes a pair, both go. A ring round your picture is your clock: 20 seconds.
  • No cards left? You’re SAFE — out of the game, and you can’t lose.
  • The Joker never pairs. Whoever is left holding it at the end loses.

Three ways to play

  • 🔁 Classic — always take from the next player. Pure luck.
  • 🎯 Free Pick — choose who to take from.
  • 🤫 Bluff — Free Pick, and you may hide one pair so you look like a safe player to rob.
