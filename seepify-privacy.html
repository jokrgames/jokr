🂠 Seepify

Privacy policy

Seepify · com.seepify.seepify · Last updated 19 September 2026

Seepify does not collect any personal data, and it cannot. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no purchases — and it holds no network permission at all, so nothing you do in it can be sent anywhere.

What the app sends

Nothing, ever. The released Android app does not declare the INTERNET permission. This is not an unused permission left in place for later: it is absent, which means Android itself will not let the app open a network connection. Seepify plays entirely on your device, against opponents that run on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Everything Seepify saves stays on your device, in the app’s own private storage, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It is one saved slot, holding the match you are in the middle of and how you like to play. Nothing here is transmitted.

WhatWhy
The match in progress — the hands, the floor, the scoreSo you can close the app mid-deal and pick it up where you left it
Difficulty and game speedSo the app opens the way you left it
Sound settingCovers sound and haptics together
Practice mode settingWhether the floor is shown before the bid

There is no display name, no profile, no avatar and no identifier of any kind in there — the app never asks who you are. If storage is unavailable on your device, the game still plays; it simply will not resume a match.

Advertising, analytics and payments

There are none. Seepify contains no advertising SDK, no analytics or telemetry SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no in-app purchases or other payments. There is no virtual currency, no coins, no entry fee, no wagering and no real-money element of any kind.

Children

Seepify is a card game suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your choices

  • Clearing what is stored: uninstalling the app removes everything it has saved. Android’s Settings → Apps → Seepify → Storage → Clear data does the same without uninstalling.
  • Requesting deletion: there is nothing held about you to delete — no account exists and no data leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the updated policy is published at the same address.
