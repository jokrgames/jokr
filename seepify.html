Not released yet

No download yet. There is no beta, no sign-up list and no APK to install — this page is here so there is something honest to point at while it is being built.

The Indian four-player partnership fishing game, played properly. You take the South seat with an AI partner across from you, against East and West, and play full matches — blind bid, compulsory capture, houses, seeps, baazis — on your own phone, offline.

Why it exists

Seep is played everywhere and yet there is no good way to simply play a hand on your own phone. The digital versions are online-first: accounts, coins, entry fees, matchmaking, ads. Seepify is the opposite of that. It is one app that deals you in, plays by the real rules, and asks nothing of you.

It is also built for the half of the table that is still learning. The rules are intricate — compulsory capture, cemented houses, the partner exception — so the app surfaces them while you play rather than in a manual you never open.

What’s being built

Rules that are correct

Every rule is sourced to a sentence on pagat.com and pinned by a test, so the awkward cases — cementing and breaking houses, the partner exception, seeps at 50/25/0 — behave the way the game actually does.

A table that shows its working

Tap a card and everything on the floor it can legally take lights up; what it can’t is dimmed. Collect, Combine and Throw are offered per card, each variant named with the exact cards — and when an action isn’t available, the table says why instead of the button doing nothing.

A partner worth having

Three difficulty levels, and the default plays sensibly: it does not hand the opposition easy seeps, it does not break its own partner’s house, and it does not take what its partner is set up to take.

Drawn in code

Not a single bundled asset. The cards, the felt, the launcher icon and every sound are generated at runtime, which is why the whole app is a small download and why it looks the same on every screen.

Offline, absolutely

This is the strongest claim either of our games can make. The release build declares no Android INTERNET permission at all — not an unused one, none. The app is not able to reach a network, so nothing about you can leave your phone even in principle.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no connection.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no identifiers.
  • No coins, entry fees, ads or purchases.
  • One save slot on your device for the match in progress, and your settings. See the privacy policy.

Where it stands
