What both games do the same way

  • They play offline. Against the computer, no connection is needed. JOKR’s online modes need one, of course.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases. No coins, no entry fees, no wagering, nothing to unlock.
  • Signing in is optional. JOKR lets you sign in with Google so your name and stats follow you to another phone; every mode works without it. No passwords of our own.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers. Each game’s privacy policy lists exactly what it keeps and why.
  • Built for the way these games are really played — with family and friends, rules and all.
