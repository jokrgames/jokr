Privacy policy

Joker Kiske Paas? · in.jokr.jokerkiskepaas · Last updated 17 October 2026

JOKR has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no purchases of any kind. Signing in is optional: you can play everything as a guest. Playing against the computer sends nothing anywhere. Playing online sends the game server only what a game needs — listed below — and the server keeps it only while the game lasts. If you sign in with Google, we keep the least we can to recognise you: Google's id for you, the name you play under, and your game statistics.

What the app stores, and where

Everything JOKR saves stays on your device, in the app's own private storage, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. None of it is transmitted, except as described under What the app sends when you play online, and — only if you sign in — your name and game statistics, under Signing in with Google.

Your display name and chosen avatarSo the game can address you by name

The display name is free text you type yourself. Nothing checks who you are, and you are free to enter anything.

What the app sends

Playing against the computer (Play with AI): nothing. That mode runs entirely on your device.

Checking for updates. When the app opens, it asks our game server one question — the oldest app version it still supports for online play — and the request carries nothing about you. Whether a newer version exists is answered by the Google Play Store app on your phone, not by us; JOKR sends us nothing for it.

Playing online — Play with Friends or Play Online — sends the game server this, and nothing else:

  • Play with Friends: the display name you chose (your friends at the table see it), the avatar you chose, the room code you created or joined, your moves in the game, and the reactions you choose to send.
  • Play Online, with people you do not know: the same — your display name (the other players see it), avatar, moves and reactions.
  • Reactions are the app's own fixed phrases, sent as a code. Nothing you type is ever sent to another player, apart from your display name.
  • The app's version number, so the server can tell a version too old for online play to update.

Server logs. Like any web server, ours records each request's IP address, time, address requested and app version, to keep the service working and secure. The game server also notes what happens — a room created, a game started or finished, a sign-in — without any name, email or account. Logs are deleted automatically after 7 days at most, are never backed up, and are not shared with anyone.

No email address, no phone number, no contacts, no location, no files, no advertising identifier and no device identifier.

How long it is kept: the game server holds a room in memory only while it is in use, and deletes it automatically within six hours of the last activity. Nothing is written to a database, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

During testing: online play currently connects to a test server on a local network, and that connection is not encrypted. Do not use a display name you would mind others on that network seeing.

Inviting someone

JOKR can open your device's own share sheet so you can tell a friend about the game. The message is a line of text and a link to the store page; you choose who receives it and which app carries it.

The app does not see the recipient, does not keep a record that you shared, and sends nothing itself — the share sheet belongs to your phone, not to JOKR. There is no referral code, no identifier and nothing in the message that could be traced back to you.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account so your display name and game statistics follow you to other phones. Google shows you its own sign-in screen and tells us who you are; we never see your Google password.

What we keep, for as long as you have an account: Google's id for you (a number that identifies your Google account to us), the display name you play under, your game statistics (how many games you have played, survived and lost, by kind of game and mode, and your streaks — counts only, not who you played or when), and when the account was created and last changed. We also keep a random sign-in key for each phone you sign in on, stored in a form that cannot be turned back into the key.

What we do not keep: your email address, photo or real name. Google includes them in what it sends at sign-in; we read only the id and discard the rest.

On your phone, the app keeps only its own sign-in key — not Google's — and a copy of your statistics to show when you are offline.

Deleting your account: Profile (tap your avatar on the home screen) → Account → Delete account removes everything above from our server immediately. Without the app, email us from the Google account you signed in with (see Contact) and we delete it within 30 days. Signing out keeps the account but ends the sign-in on that phone.

Advertising, analytics and payments

There are none. JOKR contains no advertising SDK, no analytics or telemetry SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no in-app purchases or other payments. The app has no virtual currency, no wagering and no real-money element of any kind.

Children

JOKR is a family card game. It asks no one for their age or contact details, and signing in is never required. Online, the only thing a player writes that others can see is their display name, and names on a list of offensive words are refused.

Your choices

  • Clearing what is stored: uninstalling the app removes everything it has saved. Android's Settings → Apps → JOKR → Storage → Clear data does the same without uninstalling.
  • Requesting deletion: if you signed in, delete your account as described under Signing in with Google. What online play sends is held only while a game lasts and deleted automatically within six hours, account or not.
