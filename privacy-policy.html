🃏 JOKR

Privacy policy

Joker Kiske Paas? · in.jokr.jokerkiskepaas · Last updated 18 September 2026

JOKR does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no purchases of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

Everything JOKR saves stays on your device, in the app's own private storage, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Nothing here is transmitted.

WhatWhy
Your display name and chosen avatarSo the game can address you by name
Preferences — sound, haptics, game speed, theme, default mode and player countSo the app opens the way you left it
Game statistics — games played, games survived, streaksThe stats strip on the home screen
A server address, if you change it from the defaultSo you do not have to retype it

The display name is free text you type yourself. Nothing checks who you are, and you are free to enter anything.

What the app sends

Nothing. This version of JOKR plays entirely on your device — passing one phone around, or against the built-in computer opponents. It makes no network requests at all.

The app declares the Android INTERNET permission because online play is built and will be switched on in a later version. Until then the permission is unused.

If online play is switched on later

A future version will let you play with friends over the internet. This policy will be updated before that ships, but for transparency, this is what that mode is built to send to the game server, and nothing else:

  • the display name you chose,
  • the avatar you chose,
  • the room code you created or joined,
  • your moves in the game.

No email address, no phone number, no contacts, no location, no files, no advertising identifier and no device identifier. Rooms are held only while the game is being played and expire automatically a few hours afterwards.

Advertising, analytics and payments

There are none. JOKR contains no advertising SDK, no analytics or telemetry SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no in-app purchases or other payments. The app has no virtual currency, no wagering and no real-money element of any kind.

Children

JOKR is a family card game. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your choices

  • Clearing what is stored: uninstalling the app removes everything it has saved. Android's Settings → Apps → JOKR → Storage → Clear data does the same without uninstalling.
  • Requesting deletion: there is nothing held about you to delete — no account exists and no data leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the updated policy is published at the same address.
