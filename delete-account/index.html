🃏 Delete your JOKR account

JOKR (in.jokr.jokerkiskepaas) lets you sign in with Google so your name and game statistics follow you between phones. Signing in is optional, and you can delete the account at any time.

In the app

  1. Open JOKR and tap your avatar at the top of the home screen to open your Profile.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account, then Delete.

The account is removed from our server straight away.

Without the app

Email jokr.gamify@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, asking for your JOKR account to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything we hold for the account: your Google user id, the name you play under, your game statistics, and every sign-in session. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Your settings live only on your phone. Deleting the account does not touch them; uninstalling the app removes them.
